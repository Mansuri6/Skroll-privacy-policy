Welcome to SKROLL ("the Platform", "we", "us", or "our") — an on-demand marketplace that connects clients ("Customers") with independent event professionals ("Service Providers" or "Service Providers") such as photographers, DJs, caterers, and event planners.

This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and what choices you have. By creating an account or using the SKROLL app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you give us

  • Account details: full name, email address, phone number, chosen role (Customer or Service Provider), and profile picture.
  • Service Provider business details: business name, service category (Photography, DJ, Catering, Event Planning, etc.), portfolio images, package descriptions, and pricing.
  • Bank details (Service Providers only): bank account number, bank code, and account holder name — collected only to send your earnings, and verified/transferred securely through our payment partner, Paystack.
  • Messages & quotes: content of chats between customers and service providers, agreed quotes, and booking dates.
  • Reviews: star ratings and written reviews left after a completed booking.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Location: with your permission, we use your approximate or precise foreground location to show nearby service providers and calculate event distance. We never track location in the background.
  • Push notification token (FCM): used only to deliver chat and booking alerts to your device.
  • Photos & media: accessed only when you choose to upload a profile photo, portfolio image, or chat attachment. Files are stored securely via Cloudinary.
We do not collect contacts, call logs, SMS, biometric data, or health information — full stop.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Marketplace matching: helping customers discover nearby service providers through search, map view, and browsing.
  • Bookings & escrow: managing quotes, availability, advance payments, and confirming service delivery.
  • Payouts & commission: calculating the 5% platform fee and releasing the service provider's net earnings (95%) to their verified bank account.
  • Safety & fraud prevention: reviewing user reports and enforcing our zero-tolerance policy against abuse.
  • Customer support: resolving issues and disputes via our support email.

3. AI Event Assistant & Smart Search

SKROLL includes an interactive AI Event Assistant, powered by Google Cloud / Firebase Cloud Functions (Gemini), that lets you describe what you need in plain language — for example: "I need a wedding photographer and DJ in Sandton for 150 guests with a budget under R15,000."

What the AI processes

  • The text of your search prompts, event dates, guest counts, requested service categories, and budget details.
  • This is used only to extract structured matching parameters — such as service type, location, and budget — so we can recommend relevant, verified service providers from our marketplace.

Data protection & model training

  • Your AI chat prompts are processed in real time through secure, enterprise-grade Google Cloud functions, encrypted in transit.
  • Your prompts and conversations with the AI Assistant are never used to train public AI models.
  • Your AI search queries are never sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Safety & content moderation

In line with Google Play's Generative AI policies, the AI Assistant runs under strict safety filters designed to prevent inappropriate, defamatory, or harmful responses. If you ever receive an unexpected or inappropriate AI response, you can flag it directly through our in-app reporting tool or by emailing m.mansuri@skroll.services.

4. Payments, Subscriptions & Escrow

SKROLL uses two separate, policy-compliant payment channels, depending on what is being paid for:

What Who pays Payment method
Service Provider subscription (unlocks live listing, portfolio, quotes) Service Providers only — Customers use the app for free Google Play Billing (Android) / Apple StoreKit (iOS)
Advance deposit for a real-world event booking Customers (pays gateway fee) Paystack (held in escrow until service is delivered)
Service Provider payout (95% of booking value) Paid to Service Provider Paystack Transfer API, after service is confirmed complete

Service Provider subscription terms & cancellation

  • Auto-renewal: subscriptions bill on a recurring monthly cycle and renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current billing period ends.
  • How to cancel:
    • Android: Google Play Store → Profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → SKROLL → Cancel subscription.
    • iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → SKROLL → Cancel Subscription.
  • Access after cancellation: service providers keep full listing and subscriber features until the end of the current paid billing cycle.
  • Subscription refunds: billing and refund requests for the subscription itself are handled directly under Google Play's and Apple's own refund policies, not by SKROLL.

Booking cancellations & escrow refunds

If a service provider cancels or fails to show up for a booked event, the customer receives a 100% full refund of their advance deposit — SKROLL waives its platform fee and absorbs the gateway charge. Refunds are typically processed within 3–7 business days via Paystack.

SKROLL does not store your full card number or banking password on our own servers.

5. Third-Party Services We Use

We only share data with trusted, verified partners — never with advertisers or data brokers.

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — authentication and secure database storage.
  • Paystack (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified) — payment processing, bank verification, and service provider payouts.
  • Cloudinary — secure storage for profile photos, portfolios, and chat images.
  • Google Maps Platform — displaying map locations and calculating proximity.
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any advertiser or third-party data broker.

6. Account & Data Deletion

You are always in control of your data:

  • In-app deletion: go to Settings → Delete Account, confirm your password, and your account is permanently deleted.
  • What gets deleted: your login credentials, profile, chat history, bookings, and all uploaded photos are permanently and irreversibly removed from our active systems.
  • Email request: you may also request deletion by emailing m.mansuri@skroll.services. We complete these requests within 48 business hours.

7. Reporting, Blocking & User Conduct

SKROLL has a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, fraud, and abusive behavior.

  • Report any user, message, or profile with the in-app "Report User" button.
  • Instantly block anyone — blocked users can't message you and won't appear in your search.
  • Manage blocked accounts anytime under Settings → Blocked Users.
  • Our safety team reviews every report within 24 hours and may terminate offending accounts without refund.

8. Children's Privacy

SKROLL is built for users aged 18 and older who can legally enter into service agreements. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). If we discover a minor's account, it is deleted immediately.

9. Data Security
